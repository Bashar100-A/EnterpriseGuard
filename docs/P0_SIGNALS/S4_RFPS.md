## 1. European Commission — AI Office: Technical Assistance for AI Safety

- **Issuer:** European Commission, DG CNECT — AI Office (CNECT.A.3 — AI Safety)
- **Title:** ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ACT: TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE FOR AI SAFETY
- **Reference:** TED 272332-2025
- **URL:** https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/-/detail/272332-2025
- **Published:** 2025-04-28
- **Value:** €9,080,000 (6 lots)
- **Key requirement:** Third-party contractors to support monitoring of compliance and risk assessment of General-Purpose AI (GPAI) models under EU AI Act Articles 89, 92, 93. Lot 6 = Agentic Evaluation Interface for GPAI.
- **Relevance to ADIE:** Validates that EU institutions themselves are procuring third-party technical infrastructure for AI compliance monitoring. The €9M budget and 6-lot structure show the scale of the regulatory compliance market. ADIE's independent audit layer directly addresses the "monitoring of compliance" and "risk assessment" requirements described in this RFP.

## 2. EBA + EIOPA + ESMA — Joint Statement on Frontier AI Models

- **Issuer:** Joint Committee of the European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA)
- **Title:** EBA, EIOPA and ESMA call for enhanced governance and consistent supervision to mitigate ICT risks from frontier AI models in the EU financial sector
- **URL:** https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/eba-eiopa-and-esma-call-enhanced-governance-and-consistent-supervision
- **Published:** 2026-07-31
- **Type:** Regulatory statement (NOT an RFP — included as supplementary signal)
- **Scope:** Cross-sectoral supervisory expectations for frontier AI models under DORA. Emphasizes sound governance, risk management, and oversight of critical ICT third-party providers.
- **Relevance to ADIE:** Validates that all three European financial regulators jointly recognize frontier AI models as an ICT risk requiring governance and third-party oversight under DORA. This creates regulatory demand for auditable AI decision evidence — the exact problem ADIE solves.
- **Key quote:** "The statement underlines that financial entities should have robust governance and risk management frameworks in place to support the effective management and mitigation of cyber risks associated with frontier AI models. It also updates on ongoing and planned DORA oversight activities for critical ICT third-party providers (CTPPs) to address this risk."
- **Verified:** ✅ confirmed (URL opened; quote matches page content)
