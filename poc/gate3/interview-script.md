# ADIE — Gate 3 Interview Script

**For:** 30-minute call with Internal Audit Lead
**Rule:** Listen 70%. Talk 30%.

---

## Part A — Opening (3 min)

> "Thanks for your time. I want to understand how you currently handle
> AI decision evidence during audit. I'll ask some questions, show you
> something small, and then ask what you think. No pitch."

**Ask permission:** "Can I take notes?"

---

## Part B — Current Process (5 min)

1. "Walk me through the last time you had to prepare evidence for an
   AI-driven decision during an audit."

2. "How long did it take, roughly?"

3. "Which systems did you have to pull data from?"

4. "What did you produce as the final artifact for the regulator?"

5. "Was there a moment where you had to say 'trust me' to the
   regulator?"

*(Listen. Do not correct. Do not suggest.)*

---

## Part C — Introduce (5 min)

Show one-pager, then the certificate, then run the verifier live
(if they're comfortable).

> "This is what I want to show you. It's a file, and a small script.
> Anyone can verify it offline. What it proves is integrity — nothing
> more."

---

## Part D — Reactions (10 min)

6. "What's your first reaction?"

7. "Does this map onto a problem you have?"

8. "Where would it not help?"

9. "If it worked, would you use it in your next audit?"

10. "Would your regulator accept a certificate like this?"

11. "What would you need to see before using it?"

12. "Would you pay for this? If yes, roughly what range?"

*(Silence is fine. Let them think.)*

---

## Part E — Close (7 min)

> "Thanks. If it's useful, I'd like to come back in a few weeks and
> offer a small 7-day pilot. No commitment now. Is that OK?"

If yes: take email, note "warm lead".
If no: ask "what would change your mind?", write it down.

---

## Post-Call (immediately)

Fill the response log (see response-log-template.md) **before** the
next call. Do not summarize. Use their words.

---

**End of script.**
