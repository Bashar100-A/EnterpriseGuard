# ADIE — LinkedIn Message Variants

**Rule:** Short. No jargon. No claims. Ask for opinion, not sale.

---

## Variant 1 — Cold (no prior connection)

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm researching how internal audit teams handle evidence for AI
> decisions, especially under DORA. I built a small tool that might
> help auditors verify decisions independently.
>
> Not selling anything. Would 20 minutes of your time to give an
> honest opinion be possible?
>
> Happy to send a 1-page summary first.

**When to use:** Unknown auditor, no mutual connection.
**Expected response rate:** 10–20%.

---

## Variant 2 — Warm (mutual connection mentioned)

> Hi [name],
>
> [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out. I'm working on a small
> project around audit evidence for AI decisions — DORA-relevant.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes to look at it and tell me whether it's
> actually useful? No pitch.
>
> Can send a 1-page summary if helpful.

**When to use:** Introduced by a shared contact.
**Expected response rate:** 40–60%.

---

## Variant 3 — Reply to their post

> Hi [name],
>
> Your recent post on [specific topic they wrote about] caught my
> attention. I'm working on a similar problem from a technical angle
> — making AI decision evidence independently verifiable.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation? Not selling.
> Genuinely want to understand your perspective.

**When to use:** They recently posted about DORA, AI Act, or audit.
**Expected response rate:** 30–50%.

---

## Variant 4 — After event / conference

> Hi [name],
>
> We both attended [event name]. I was struck by your comment on
> [specific thing they said].
>
> I'm working on something adjacent — a way for auditors to verify
> AI decisions without needing the bank's cooperation. Would you
> have 20 minutes to talk?

**When to use:** You saw them at a conference or webinar.
**Expected response rate:** 50–70%.

---

## Variant 5 — Reconnect (dormant connection)

> Hi [name],
>
> It's been a while since we last spoke. I hope you're well.
>
> I'm working on something in your domain — audit evidence for AI
> decisions, DORA-relevant. Would you be open to a short
> conversation? Not selling, just want your perspective.

**When to use:** You know them from years ago.
**Expected response rate:** 40–60%.

---

## Rules for All Variants

- Never say "startup", "revolutionary", "disruptive", or "game-changing".
- Never use the words "blockchain", "AI-powered", or "next-gen".
- Never include a link in the first message.
- Never ask for more than 20 minutes.
- Always give them an easy exit ("no pressure").

---

## Follow-Up (if no reply after 5 days)

Send exactly one follow-up:

> Hi [name], just following up on my message from last week. If the
> timing isn't right, no problem — happy to reconnect later.
>
> Thanks either way.

**No more than one follow-up.** Ever.
