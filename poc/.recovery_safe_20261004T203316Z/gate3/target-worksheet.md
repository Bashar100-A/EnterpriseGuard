## Search Filters (LinkedIn)

**Exact strings to search:**
- "Internal Audit" bank
- "Head of Internal Audit" bank
- "Audit Manager" AI OR DORA
- "Technology Audit" EU bank

**Location filters:** Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, Spain, Italy.

**Company filters:** Banks in EU (Deutsche Bank, BNP, ING, ABN AMRO, BBVA, Santander, Intesa, etc.).
